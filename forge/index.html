No music so loud you can't hear yourself think. No upselling on products you don't need. Just a proper cut, a proper chat, and a brew if you fancy one.
— Dave, on what makes Forge different

Forge was started in 2013 by Dave Crowley, a Shiregreen lad who learned his trade in Leeds and brought it home. Twelve years later we’re still on Bellhouse Road, still doing it the way it should be done.

Three chairs. Three barbers who actually know their craft.
